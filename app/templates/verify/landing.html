How BlockCert Guarantees Authenticity

The blockchain is the immutable source of truth — never trust, always verify.

1

Cryptographic Fingerprint

When an administrator issues a certificate, the system generates a SHA-256 hash directly from the raw document bytes.

2

On-Chain Anchor

The certificate ID and hash are anchored immutably onto the Solidity smart contract. No PII is stored on-chain.

3

Real-Time Verification

Employers scan the QR or search the ID. The file hash is recomputed on the fly and matched against the blockchain record.
